# Integrations

Other mods add to Storage when installed. With AE2 or Refined Storage, a [network bridge](bridges.md) links their
networks with yours.

With Curios, carried gadgets also work from its slots: the <ItemLink id="maxtech_storage:restock_belt" /> in the belt
slot, and the <ItemLink id="maxtech_storage:wireless_terminal" />, <ItemLink id="maxtech_storage:crafting_tablet" />,
<ItemLink id="maxtech_storage:logic_remote" />, <ItemLink id="maxtech_storage:pocket_uplink" /> and
<ItemLink id="maxtech_storage:portable_storage_cell" /> in the charm slot.

Bind **Open a carried gadget** (controls, MaxTech Storage gadgets) to open one from a slot.
